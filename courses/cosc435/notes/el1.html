EL-01: The Shape of a Dot Product

Engaged Learning · 1% of course grade

Why This Matters

On the first day of class you heard: “Every song is described by 64 numbers. We measure how well two things match by multiplying those numbers together and adding up.” That operation is a dot product — and it is the computational heart of your capstone.

This assignment gives you the visual intuition that makes the math feel obvious rather than arbitrary. You will need it in Module 4 (tiled matrix multiply) and in the capstone.

What to Watch

3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra (YouTube playlist: “Essence of linear algebra” by 3Blue1Brown)

Watch these three episodes in order — they are short (8–10 min each):

  1. Episode 1 — “Vectors, what even are they?” What a vector is, how to picture it, why mathematicians and physicists define it differently.

  2. Episode 3 — “Linear transformations and matrices” What it means to multiply a matrix by a vector. The visual that makes matrix math click.

  3. Episode 9 — “Dot products and duality” The geometric meaning of the dot product: projection, alignment, and why a value near 1.0 means “pointing in the same direction.”

Total viewing time: approximately 30 minutes.

Reflection Prompts

Write a 200–300 word reflection on Blackboard for EL-01. Address all three prompts — a sentence or two each is fine, but be specific:

1. The geometric picture. In your own words, what does a dot product measure geometrically? (Not the formula — the meaning. Use the word “direction” somewhere in your answer.)

2. The similarity connection. Suppose two songs both have 64-number embeddings and those vectors point in nearly the same direction. What does their dot product tell you about how similar the songs are? Why does normalising the vectors to unit length make this cleaner?

3. Your honest reaction. Did anything in the videos surprise you, change how you think about something, or clarify something that felt like magic before? If the videos were old news to you, describe one thing you noticed in the visualisations that you had not thought about that way before.
